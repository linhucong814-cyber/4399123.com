# Code of Conduct

4399123.com is a community for builders, beginners, AI tool explorers, designers, writers, and developers.

We want this space to feel open, respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Welcome beginners.
- Give specific and constructive feedback.
- Assume people are learning.
- Credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions focused on building and improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Hate speech
- Publicly sharing private information
- Mocking people for being new
- Posting secrets, credentials, or private user data
- Repeated spam or off-topic promotion

## Reporting

If you see behavior that harms the community, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
